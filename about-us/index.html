About Us

Team Volt Esports Hub is a competitive esports platform that organizes skill-based tournaments and scrims for mobile esports titles such as Free Fire.

We host daily and scheduled competitive matches across multiple time slots, including practice scrims, custom room tournaments, and structured multi-match formats. Our platform supports both free and paid competitions, allowing players of all levels to participate and improve their gameplay.

We provide a structured match environment that includes lobby hosting, scheduling, rankings, and result management. Our system ensures fair competition through transparent rules, organized formats, and performance-based scoring systems.

Team Volt Esports Hub does not own, publish, or modify any game. We operate solely as an independent esports tournament organizer using official in-game custom rooms.

All competitions are based on player skill, teamwork, strategy, and in-game performance. Rewards may be distributed to top-performing teams based on match results and verification. Participation does not guarantee any reward.

We regularly organize multiple lobbies and tournaments every day, creating consistent opportunities for players to compete, practice, and grow within the esports ecosystem.

Our goal is to support competitive players, build a strong esports community, and provide a reliable platform for organized esports competition.
