Payment & Participation Policy

01 Purpose of Entry Fees

Team Volt Esports Hub charges entry fees strictly for slot booking and esports service operations. The entry fee is collected to cover:

  • Custom room and lobby hosting
  • Match scheduling, admin operations, and moderation
  • Fair-play monitoring and anti-cheat enforcement

The entry fee is NOT a bet, wager, or stake on match outcomes.

02 Skill-Based Competition

All scrims and tournaments hosted by Team Volt Esports Hub are skill-based esports competitions. Match results depend entirely on player skill, strategy, teamwork, and in-game performance. No element of chance, luck, or randomness is involved. No guaranteed winnings or returns are promised.

03 Refund & Cancellation Policy

Once a slot is successfully booked and confirmed, the entry fee is non-refundable. No refunds for no-shows, late joining, or disqualification.

If a match is cancelled by the organizer, the fee will be refunded or adjusted to the next lobby.

04 Age & Payment Responsibility

Participation is allowed for players aged 16 years and above. All payments and registrations must be completed by a parent, legal guardian, or team representative aged 18 years or above.
