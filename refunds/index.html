Refund Policy

Entry Fee Refund:

Once a slot is confirmed, participation fees are non-refundable.

Event Cancellation:

If a match is cancelled by the organizer, the fee will be refunded or adjusted to a future event.

Payment Issues:

If payment is completed but registration is not confirmed, users must contact support with transaction details.

Refund Timeline:

Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 working days.
